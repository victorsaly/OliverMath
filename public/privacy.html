OliverMath

Privacy for parents

Last updated 21 November 2026

The short version. Oliver Math is a maths game for children. It has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. We do not know who your child is, and we do not collect their name, age, email, location or photos.

The game listens to your child's spoken answer. Where that sound goes depends on which version you use, and this page says exactly where.

Your child's voice

VersionWhere the voice goes
iPhone, iPad and Android app It stays on the device. The phone or tablet turns the spoken answer into a number by itself, and the sound is not recorded, saved or sent to us or to anyone else.
Web version, in a browser While the microphone is open, the sound is sent to Microsoft's Azure Speech service, which turns it into text and sends the text back to the game. The sound does not pass through our servers, and Oliver Math does not record or keep it.

If you would rather no sound left the device at all, use the app, or play the web version with the number pad: tap 123 and your child can tap the answer instead of saying it. The microphone can be refused or switched off at any time and the game still works.

The first time the app is used in a language, the phone downloads a speech model for that language from Apple or Google. That is a download to the device; nothing about your child is sent.

Microsoft handles what it receives under its own terms: Microsoft Privacy Statement.

What is kept, and where

The game remembers a few things so your child can carry on where they left off. All of it is stored on the device, in the app or the browser, and none of it is sent to us:

  • stars, best streak and badges;
  • the sums that were asked and whether each answer was right;
  • settings: language, level, type of sum, sound, and the robot's colour.

To remove the record of past sums, open Grown-ups (the padlock) and choose Clear history. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes everything, including stars.

What reaches a server

The app works without a connection and contacts no server while your child plays.

The web version is delivered by GitHub Pages and uses a small service of ours, hosted on Microsoft Azure, for two things: to let the browser talk to Azure Speech, and to make the robot's voice. To make the voice, the text of the question (for example "What is 7 times 8?") is sent to that service. Nothing your child says or types is included.

As with any website, the companies that host these services see the address your device connects from (its IP address) and may log it for security. We do not use those logs to identify or follow anyone.

What the game leaves out

  • It shows no adverts.
  • It uses no analytics, tracking or cookies that follow your child.
  • It has no accounts, sign-ups or in-app purchases.
  • It does not sell or share data, and holds none it could sell.
  • It gives your child no links out of the game. The links to this page and to support sit behind the Grown-ups gate.

Children's privacy

Oliver Math is made for children of seven and up, to be set up by a parent or guardian. The game does not collect personal information from children, so there is nothing for us to hold on your behalf or delete on request.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects any of this, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
