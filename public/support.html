OliverMath

Help and support

For parents and guardians

How to play. Tap Play. The robot asks a sum out loud and shows it in big figures. Your child says the answer. A right answer earns stars.

The robot cannot hear my child

  • Check the microphone is allowed. If the game shows "I can't hear you yet", the microphone has been refused or is switched off.
    • iPhone and iPad: Settings → Oliver Math → Microphone.
    • Android: Settings → Apps → Oliver Math → Permissions → Microphone.
    • In a browser: use the site settings beside the address to allow the microphone for this site.
  • Say just the number. "Fifty-six" works better than a long sentence. Wait for the green ring and "Your turn!" before speaking.
  • Try a quieter room. Music, a television or other children talking make short answers hard to hear.
  • Use the number pad. Tap 123 and your child can tap the answer. It earns the same stars. After two answers that were not heard, the pad opens by itself.

The app says it needs the internet once

The app listens with a speech model that lives on the phone. The first time a language is used, the phone has to download that model. Connect to the internet once and press Play; after that the game works with no connection. Until then your child can play with the number pad.

Some phones do not have a model for every language. If yours does not, the game will say it cannot listen on this device, and the number pad still works.

Changing the level, the type of sum or the language

Tap the padlock at the top left. The game asks you to tap three numbers written as words; this keeps settings out of small hands. Inside you can choose:

  • Level: Easy, Medium, Hard, or Auto, which adjusts to how your child is doing.
  • Sums: times, plus, minus or divided by.
  • Language: English, Spanish, French, German or Portuguese. Questions are asked, and answers are heard, in the language you pick.
  • Sound, the robot's colour and view, and day or night.

Stars and progress

Tap the stars at the top right to see sums answered, best run and badges. Progress is kept on the device only, so it does not follow your child to another phone or browser.

To remove the record of past sums, open Grown-ups and choose Clear history. Deleting the app or clearing the site's data removes everything, including stars.

The free version and the full game

  • Free: ten sums a day, every day. When they are used up the robot says "That's all for today!" and they come back the next day.
  • Full game: one purchase removes the limit for everyone who shares the device. It is not a subscription.
  • To unlock: open Grown-ups (the padlock), pass the gate, and choose Unlock the full game.
  • New phone, or reinstalled? Open the same screen and choose Restore purchase. You are not charged again.
  • A refund is requested from Apple or Google, who took the payment.

Is it safe for my child?

There are no adverts, no accounts and no links out of the game, and the one purchase the app offers is behind the Grown-ups gate. In the app, your child's voice never leaves the device. Privacy for parents explains this in full, including how the web version differs.

Get in touch

If something is not working, or you have a question about the game, write to us through the project's page: github.com/victorsaly/OliverMath/issues. Please do not include your child's name or any personal details.
